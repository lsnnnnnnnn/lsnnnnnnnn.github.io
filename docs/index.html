Sheng’en Li

CV / GitHub / Email

I am an undergraduate in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science in the dual-degree program at Duke Kunshan University and Duke University.

I study how to evaluate and improve learning and decision systems under feedback, temporal dependence, and modeling uncertainty. My work spans counterfactual evaluation, sequential detection, and numerical verification.

I also write research notes about the questions and design choices behind my work, and build research software. My latest note explains why I built Reader First, a writing skill for making technical explanations easier to follow.
